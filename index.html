Common questions

What does SAMA do?

SAMA is a web design and development studio based in Beirut, Lebanon. We design and build websites, online stores, mobile apps and interfaces, and we edit video for brands.

Are the websites on this page real?

They are working demos that we designed and built, using fictional businesses. You can open each one and try it on desktop, tablet or mobile, then ask us to make a version for your own business.

How much does a website cost in Lebanon?

It depends on the pages and features you need. We send one fixed quote within 24 hours of hearing about your project, and that price does not grow halfway through.

Can you make my website appear on Google?

Yes, we set up every site so Google can read and understand it, and we show you how to verify it in Search Console. No honest studio can guarantee a first position, but a fast and clear site gives you a strong start.

Do you only work with businesses in Lebanon?

No. We are based in Beirut and work with clients anywhere, with calls and previews handled online.
