SEO services in Lebanon

We help your business show up when people in Lebanon search for what you sell. That means fixing what stops Google from reading your site, writing pages around the words your customers use, and setting up your Google Business Profile.

Start by finding out what is wrong

We begin with an audit of your site: can Google crawl it, are pages indexed, how fast does it load on a phone, and which searches already bring you visitors. You get a short list of problems ranked by how much they matter, so the first work goes where it will help most.

Local SEO for businesses in Lebanon

Searches like "dentist in Beirut" or "restaurant near me" often show a map before any website. We set up and complete your Google Business Profile, make sure your name, address and phone match everywhere they appear, and show you how to ask real customers for reviews. Many searches here also happen in Arabic, so we check which language your customers actually use before writing anything.

Pages written around real searches

A page ranks when it answers what someone typed. We research the words people use for your service and city, then write or improve your titles, descriptions, headings and content to match. The writing stays natural and useful, because a page that sounds like a keyword list loses customers even if it gets the click.

What we will never promise

Nobody can honestly guarantee first place on Google, because Google decides and your competitors matter. We also avoid shortcuts such as buying links or copying content, since they can get a site penalised. SEO usually takes months to show results, and we report what actually changed using your own Search Console data.

  • Keyword research for your market
  • Titles, descriptions and page content
  • Structured data and sitemaps
  • Google Business Profile setup
  • Search Console setup and reports

FAQ

Common questions

How long does SEO take to work?

Technical fixes can help within weeks, once Google has crawled your pages again. Ranking for competitive searches usually takes several months, and it depends on your market and competitors.

Can you guarantee first place on Google?

No, and be careful with anyone who does. We can promise honest work, correct technical setup, useful content and clear reports on what changed.

Do you do SEO for a website you didn't build?

Yes. We start with an audit of your current site and tell you what can be fixed where it is, and whether anything needs rebuilding.

What do you need from me to start?

Your website address, what you sell and where, and access to Google Search Console if you already have it. If you don't, we can set it up with you.
