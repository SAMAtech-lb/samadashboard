About SAMA

A small team with a big sky.

SAMA (سماء, Arabic for sky) is a creative tech studio in Beirut, Lebanon. We design and build websites, mobile apps and interfaces, and we edit video, all in one team.

Why one team matters

When design, code and content sit in different places, decisions slow down and brands start to look different from one channel to the next. Keeping them together means faster answers and a business that looks the same on its website, its app and its reels.

Who is behind SAMA

SAMA was founded by Ahmad Ghzayel, a full stack developer with a BSc in Computer Science from the Lebanese International University and an Odoo certification. When you work with us, you can talk directly to the person building your project.

How we like to work

We prefer to be plain about things. You get a fixed quote before work begins, a preview link every week and straight answers, including when a smaller project would serve you better than the one you first asked about.

Why you can try our work first

Everything in our library is a working build, not a picture of one. The businesses in the demos are fictional, but the booking flows, filters and checkouts are real code you can click through on your own phone before you ever talk to us.
