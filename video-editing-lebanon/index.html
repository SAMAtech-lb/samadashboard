Video editing services in Lebanon

Send us your footage and we will turn it into reels, ads, YouTube videos and brand films. We handle the cut, colour, captions, motion graphics and sound, so your story lands in the first three seconds.

Edited for where it will be watched

A vertical reel and a long YouTube video follow different rules. Short video needs an immediate hook and a quick pace in 9:16, while long-form video has room to breathe in 16:9. We edit for the platform and export the correct formats, so you do not have one clip cropped badly into every shape.

Captions, because many people watch on mute

Lots of people scroll with the sound off. Clear, well-timed captions keep them watching and make the video easier to understand. We style them to match your brand instead of using the same default look as everyone else.

Your brand in every frame

Consistent colours, titles and logo animations make your videos recognisable before anyone reads your name. If we also build your website or app, your reels and your site will look like they come from the same company.

How it works

Tell us the goal, the length and where the video will go, then share your footage through a link. We send back a first cut, take your notes, and deliver the final files for each platform. We edit in Premiere Pro and After Effects.

What's included

  • Reels, TikTok and YouTube Shorts
  • YouTube videos and brand films
  • Ads and promo videos
  • Motion graphics, logos and titles
  • Colour grading and sound clean-up
  • Captions for watching without sound

Common questions

How do I send you my footage?

A shared link from Google Drive, Dropbox or WeTransfer works well. Send original files where you can, because videos forwarded through messaging apps lose quality.

Do you also film videos?

Our service is editing and motion graphics, so we work from footage that you supply or that you already have.

Can you edit a monthly batch of reels?

Yes. Tell us how many videos you post each month and we will give you a fixed quote for the batch.
