SAMA · Beirut, Lebanon

Web design company in Beirut, Lebanon

We design and build websites for businesses in Lebanon and abroad: company sites, landing pages, portfolios and online stores. You get one fixed quote, a live preview every week and a site that is ready for Google on launch day.

A website built for the people searching for you

Most of your visitors will arrive on a phone, often on a mobile connection, and decide within seconds whether to stay. So we start with what they came to find, keep pages light, and test on real screen sizes before you see anything. A clear page that opens quickly does more for your business than any effect on top of it.

What makes a site show up on Google

Search engines need to read your pages, understand what you do and where you are, and trust the result. We build every page as real, readable HTML with a clear title, description and headings. We add structured data for your business, a sitemap and working social previews. We also write pages around the words your customers actually use, such as your service and your city, instead of slogans. Ranking takes time and depends on your market too, so we explain what will help after launch, including your Google Business Profile and getting other sites to link to you.

English, Arabic or both

Many businesses in Lebanon serve customers in more than one language. We can plan your site in English, in Arabic with right-to-left layout, or in both, with each language on its own address so Google can show the right version to the right person.

You know the price before we start

We start with a free call about your goals, audience and budget. You leave it with a clear plan and a fixed quote that does not grow halfway through. You review the design before any code is written, then follow the build through a live preview link every week.

Common questions

How much does a website cost in Lebanon?

It depends on the number of pages and what the site has to do. A one-page landing site costs far less than a store with online checkout. Tell us what you need and we will reply within 24 hours with a fixed quote, so the price is agreed before work starts.

How long does it take to build a website?

It depends on the size of the site and how quickly content and approvals arrive. We give you a timeline with your quote, and you can follow progress through a live preview link every week.

Will my website rank first on Google?

Nobody can honestly promise a first position, because Google decides and your competitors matter. What we can promise is a fast site with correct technical SEO and clear content, plus a plan for what to do after launch.

Do you work with clients outside Lebanon?

Yes. We are based in Beirut and work with clients anywhere. Calls, previews and approvals all happen online.
