SAMA · Beirut, Lebanon

UI/UX design studio in Lebanon

We plan how a website or app should work, then design every screen in Figma. You see and approve it all before development begins, when changes are still quick and cheap.

UX is how it works, UI is how it looks

UX design decides what a person needs to do and the shortest route to doing it. UI design is the visual layer on top: type, colour, spacing and buttons. A product needs both. A beautiful screen that confuses people still loses customers, and we look at those problems first.

Test the idea before paying to build it

A clickable prototype feels like the finished product and takes far less time to make. You can show it to customers, partners or investors, watch where people get stuck and fix it while it is still only a design.

A review of what you already have

If your site or app is live but people drop off, we can go through the main journeys and show you where and why it happens. You receive a clear list of fixes ranked by impact, which your own team or ours can then work through.

Design and code under one roof

Because the same team designs and builds, what you approve is what ships. Screens are designed with real content and real devices in mind, so details do not get lost when the work moves into development.

What's included

  • User flows and wireframes
  • Interface design in Figma
  • Interactive prototypes
  • Design systems
  • Usability reviews of existing products
  • Developer handoff, or the full build with us

Common questions

What do I receive at the end?

You receive the Figma files, a clickable prototype and any design system created for the project. If we also build it, you get the working product too.

Can you redesign an existing website or app?

Yes. We usually start with a short review of what works and what does not, so the redesign fixes real problems rather than only changing how things look.

Do you work with our developers?

Yes. We can hand designs over to your team with clear specifications, or we can build the project ourselves.
