E-commerce websites for shops in Lebanon

We build online stores that make it easy to find a product and finish an order on a phone. Your range stays in one place, instead of being buried in social media posts and answered one message at a time.

Sell beyond your Instagram page

Social media is good for being discovered, but poor for browsing. Customers cannot filter by size, compare two products or see what is in stock without sending a message. A store handles those questions all day, and each product gets its own page that can appear on Google when someone searches for it.

Checkout that fits how you take orders

Payment and delivery differ from one business to another, especially in Lebanon. Before we build, we go through how you take money today and how orders reach customers. Then we plan checkout around that, whether it means cash on delivery, orders confirmed on WhatsApp, or card payment through a provider available to your business.

Photos and speed go together

A store is only as good as its product photos, and large photos are the most common reason a store feels slow. We prepare images in modern formats at the right sizes, so products look sharp while pages stay quick on a phone.

Try the demos yourself

Our store demos are real builds you can click through, with filters, product views, a cart and checkout. Open one on your phone to see how it feels for a customer, then tell us what to change for your own range.

What's included

  • Product catalogue and categories
  • Search and filters
  • Product pages with sizes and options
  • Cart and checkout flow
  • Order options planned around your business
  • Product SEO and structured data

Common questions

Can customers pay cash on delivery?

Yes, we can build that into checkout. We agree on how you take payment before the build starts, including cash on delivery, WhatsApp confirmation or card payment where a suitable provider is available to you.

Can I add and edit products myself?

That depends on the setup we agree for your store. If you plan to change products often, tell us at the start so we can include a way for you to manage them without calling a developer.

Can you move my shop from Instagram to a website?

Yes. Send us your products, prices and photos, and we will organise them into categories with a page for each product.
