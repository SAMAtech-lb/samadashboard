Stride Physio: physio clinic website

Live preview →I want one like this

About this design

Physiotherapy & sports injury clinic with a live next-available strip in the hero, conditions explorer, per-session price list, 4-step booking wizard (treatment, physio, day & time, details) and a bookable Pilates timetable.

Who it suits

This design suits a local business whose customers compare options before they call. Services, prices or listings are easy to find, and booking or asking a question takes only a few taps.

Making it yours

Stride Physio is a fictional business that we created to show this design working. For a real project, we replace the name, photos, wording and colours with your own and adjust the features to fit how you work. The price is agreed in a fixed quote before we start.
