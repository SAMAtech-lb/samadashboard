Brightbyte: electronics website

Live preview →I want one like this

About this design

A bright multi-brand electronics store (Apple, Samsung, Sony, PlayStation, Xbox, Nintendo, Canon, DJI): live search with keyboard suggestions, hero slider, category grid, deal of the day with countdown and stock bar, tabbed best-sellers with brand chips and sorting, product pop-up with storage/colour variants, Care+ cover and 0% finance, a 3-product compare tool, an instant trade-in valuer that adds basket credit, and a basket drawer.

Who it suits

This design suits a shop that wants to sell directly instead of only through social media. Customers can browse the full range, choose a size or option, and finish the order on their phone.

Making it yours

Brightbyte is a fictional business that we created to show this design working. For a real project, we replace the name, photos, wording and colours with your own and adjust the features to fit how you work. The price is agreed in a fixed quote before we start.
