Orra: fintech website

Live preview →I want one like this

About this design

A dark fintech business-account landing: a 3D metal card that tilts with the cursor with holographic sheen and colour switcher, live payment notifications and an FX ticker, a sticky phone whose app screen changes per feature on scroll, a typed currency converter showing what your bank keeps, plans, an animated vault security section, reviews and sign-up.

Who it suits

This layout suits a company with one clear offer and paid ads or social posts sending people to it. The message comes first, the proof follows, and there is always a way to get in touch within reach.

Making it yours

Orra is a fictional business that we created to show this design working. For a real project, we replace the name, photos, wording and colours with your own and adjust the features to fit how you work. The price is agreed in a fixed quote before we start.
