Rossa: pizzeria website

Live preview →I want one like this

About this design

Neapolitan wood-fired pizzeria with a spinning-pizza hero, delivery ZIP checker, live oven status, menu tabs with online ordering, a build-your-own pizza made from real photographed bases and toppings with whole or half placement, table booking and pizza school.

Who it suits

This design suits a restaurant or café that wants a menu people can read on a phone, plus ordering or booking that does not depend on someone answering messages at a busy hour.

Making it yours

Rossa is a fictional business that we created to show this design working. For a real project, we replace the name, photos, wording and colours with your own and adjust the features to fit how you work. The price is agreed in a fixed quote before we start.
