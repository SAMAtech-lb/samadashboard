Atlas Labs: software company website

Live preview →I want one like this

About this design

A premium site for a global software development company: dark hero with a custom-drawn 3D dotted globe (real coastlines, glowing flight arcs between offices, pulsing markers, drag to spin), services, filterable 'Our work' case studies with full case-study pop-ups, stats band, an interactive office globe that flies to each city with live local times, clickable 5-step process, 'Our team' with leadership bios and department filter, tech stack, Clutch reviews slider and a project-brief form.

Who it suits

This layout suits a company with one clear offer and paid ads or social posts sending people to it. The message comes first, the proof follows, and there is always a way to get in touch within reach.

Making it yours

Atlas Labs is a fictional business that we created to show this design working. For a real project, we replace the name, photos, wording and colours with your own and adjust the features to fit how you work. The price is agreed in a fixed quote before we start.
